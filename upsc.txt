UPSC Basic Information
UPSC Full Form: UPSC stands for Union Public Service Commission, which is India's central recruiting agency responsible for conducting examinations for various government services.
UPSC Establishment Year: UPSC was established on 1 October 1926 as the Public Service Commission. After independence, it was established as a constitutional body under the Indian Constitution on 26 January 1950.
UPSC Headquarters Location: The headquarters of UPSC is located at Dholpur House, Shahjahan Road, New Delhi, India.
Constitutional Status of UPSC: UPSC is a constitutional body established under Part XIV, Chapter II of the Indian Constitution.
Article Related to UPSC: Articles 315 to 323 of the Indian Constitution describe the structure, powers, functions, and independence of UPSC.
UPSC Chairman Details: The UPSC is headed by a Chairman appointed by the President of India. The Chairman leads the commission and supervises examination activities.
UPSC Functions and Responsibilities: UPSC conducts recruitment examinations, advises governments on personnel matters, manages selection processes, and ensures fair recruitment for civil services.
Types of Exams Conducted by UPSC
Civil Services Examination (CSE): UPSC conducts CSE to recruit officers for services like IAS, IPS, IFS, and other central services.
Indian Administrative Service (IAS) Examination: IAS officers are selected through UPSC CSE and are responsible for administration, policy implementation, and governance.
Indian Police Service (IPS) Examination: IPS officers manage law enforcement, internal security, and police administration.
Indian Foreign Service (IFS) Examination: IFS officers represent India in international relations, diplomacy, and foreign affairs.
Indian Forest Service (IFoS) Examination: IFoS officers manage forest resources, wildlife conservation, and environmental protection.
Engineering Services Examination (ESE): ESE recruits engineers for technical positions in government departments.
Combined Defence Services Examination (CDS): CDS is conducted for recruitment into the Indian Military Academy, Naval Academy, Air Force Academy, and Officers Training Academy.
National Defence Academy Examination (NDA): NDA examination selects candidates after Class 12 for training in Army, Navy, and Air Force.
Central Armed Police Forces Examination (CAPF): CAPF recruits Assistant Commandants for forces like BSF, CRPF, CISF, ITBP, and SSB.
Combined Medical Services Examination (CMS): CMS recruits medical officers for government healthcare services.
UPSC Civil Services Examination Structure
UPSC CSE Exam Stages: The Civil Services Examination consists of three stages:
Preliminary Examination
Main Examination
Personality Test/Interview
Preliminary Examination Details: The preliminary stage is an objective-type screening examination consisting of two papers: General Studies Paper I and CSAT Paper II.
Main Examination Details: The Main examination is a descriptive written examination that evaluates candidates' knowledge, analytical ability, and writing skills.
Personality Test/Interview Details: The interview evaluates personality, decision-making ability, communication skills, leadership qualities, and suitability for civil services.
UPSC Exam Pattern
Preliminary Exam Pattern:
General Studies Paper I: 100 questions, 200 marks
CSAT Paper II: 80 questions, 200 marks
Duration: 2 hours each paper
Main Exam Pattern: The Main examination consists of:
Essay Paper
Four General Studies Papers
Two Optional Subject Papers
Two Language Papers
Interview Marks and Weightage: The Personality Test carries 275 marks and is added to Main examination marks for final ranking.
Total Marks of UPSC Examination:
Main Examination: 1750 marks
Interview: 275 marks
Total Final Merit Marks: 2025 marks
Examination Language Options: UPSC allows candidates to write exams in English or any language listed in the Eighth Schedule of the Indian Constitution.
Exam Duration: The complete UPSC selection process generally takes around one year from notification to final result.
UPSC Eligibility Criteria
Nationality Criteria: Candidates must generally be citizens of India for IAS and IPS services. Different nationality rules apply for other services.
Age Limit for General Category: The minimum age is 21 years and maximum age is 32 years for General category candidates.
Age Limit for OBC Category: OBC candidates receive relaxation of 3 years, allowing maximum age up to 35 years.
Age Limit for SC/ST Category: SC/ST candidates receive relaxation of 5 years, allowing maximum age up to 37 years.
Age Relaxation Rules: Age relaxation is provided to reserved categories, persons with disabilities, and certain government employees according to UPSC rules.
Educational Qualification Required: Candidates must have a bachelor's degree from a recognized university.
Minimum Graduation Requirement: A candidate must complete graduation in any discipline.
Final Year Student Eligibility: Final-year graduation students can apply provisionally but must provide proof of qualification before the final stage.
Number of Attempts Allowed
Attempts for General Category: General category candidates can attempt UPSC CSE 6 times.
Attempts for OBC Category: OBC candidates can attempt UPSC CSE 9 times.
Attempts for SC/ST Category: SC/ST candidates have unlimited attempts until reaching the maximum age limit.
UPSC Syllabus Details
Preliminary General Studies Paper I Syllabus:
Current Affairs
History
Geography
Indian Polity
Economy
Environment
Science and Technology
Preliminary CSAT Paper II Syllabus:
Logical Reasoning
Analytical Ability
Reading Comprehension
Basic Mathematics
Data Interpretation
Indian History and Culture Topics:
Includes ancient history, medieval history, modern Indian history, freedom struggle, art, culture, architecture, and traditions.
Indian Geography Topics:
Includes physical geography, climate, rivers, agriculture, natural resources, industries, and human geography.
Indian Polity and Governance Topics:
Includes Constitution, Parliament, Judiciary, Fundamental Rights, Directive Principles, Panchayati Raj, and government policies.
Indian Economy Topics:
Includes economic growth, budgeting, banking, taxation, inflation, financial institutions, and government schemes.
Environment and Ecology Topics:
Includes biodiversity, climate change, pollution, environmental laws, wildlife conservation, and sustainable development.
Science and Technology Topics:
Includes space technology, biotechnology, artificial intelligence, defense technology, and scientific developments.
Current Affairs Preparation Areas:
Candidates study national and international events, government schemes, international relations, awards, reports, and important developments.
Main Examination Optional Subjects and Essay Paper Details:
Candidates choose one optional subject consisting of two papers. The Essay paper tests analytical thinking and writing ability.
Additional UPSC Dataset Categories
UPSC Notification Date: UPSC releases the Civil Services Examination notification usually in February every year.
Application Process: Candidates apply online through the UPSC official website by completing registration, filling forms, uploading documents, and paying fees.
Registration Procedure: Registration involves creating an account, entering personal details, selecting exam preferences, and submitting the application.
Application Fee: General and OBC male candidates generally pay an application fee, while female, SC, ST, and PwBD candidates are usually exempt.
Admit Card Details: UPSC releases admit cards online before each examination stage.
Exam Centers: UPSC conducts examinations in various cities across India.
Cut-off Marks: Cut-offs are decided based on candidate performance, difficulty level, and vacancy numbers.
Previous Year Question Papers: Previous papers help candidates understand exam patterns, difficulty levels, and important topics.
Recommended Books: Popular resources include NCERT textbooks, standard reference books, newspapers, and UPSC-specific preparation materials.
NCERT Preparation Strategy: Many aspirants begin preparation with NCERT books to build basic concepts in history, geography, economics, and science.
Coaching Institutes: Many institutes provide classroom and online coaching for UPSC preparation.
Online Preparation Platforms: Online platforms provide video lectures, mock tests, current affairs analysis, and study materials.
Interview Preparation: Candidates practice mock interviews, current affairs discussions, personality development, and communication skills.
Rank Calculation: Final rank is calculated using marks obtained in the Main Examination and Personality Test.
Service Allocation Process: Services like IAS, IPS, and IFS are allocated based on rank, category, preferences, and available vacancies.
DAF (Detailed Application Form): DAF contains personal information, educational details, achievements, hobbies, and service preferences used during the interview stage.
